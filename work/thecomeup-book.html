THE COME UP group show at @sadelosangeles curated by @bigpictureslosangeles and myself.

The Come Up exhibition explored moments of sudden ascent—when chance, risk, and intuition converge to alter one’s path. While the phrase often evokes ideas of hustle, heists, or lucky breaks, it also gestures toward unexpected enlightenment. Through painting, sculpture, and drawing, the exhibition traces encounters with treasures, sometimes taken, sometimes found, and the strength and insight gained through such moments.
